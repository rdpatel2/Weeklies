🎯 The Problem & Market

Busy students and professionals spend 45+ minutes every week coordinating dinners across apps, calculating budgets manually, and filtering dietary constraints.

  • Over-budget creep: Small miscalculations exceed weekly food budgets.
  • Rigid subscriptions: Standard meal boxes offer all-or-nothing plans without granular single-item customization.
  • Allergen safety: Cross-restaurant allergen checking is tedious and error-prone.
🔬 M0: The Risk-Killer Eval
